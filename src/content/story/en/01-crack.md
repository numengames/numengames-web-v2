There was a time when people truly met.

No processes. No structures. **Stories.**

But something changed. Events started working better… and meaning less. People attended more gatherings… and remembered less.

<p class="beat">They coincided… without ever connecting.</p>

It is not a lack of talent. It is not a lack of technology. It is the lack of something older. Something that is not taught: it is lived.

<p class="beat">It is not a technology problem. It is an experience problem.</p>
